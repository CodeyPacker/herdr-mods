  "We release every six weeks, on a Friday evening.

   On Thursday we stop merging and combine all the feature branches.
   That usually takes a day, because they conflict.

   Two of us follow the release document. It has 34 steps.
   Last time step 19 had changed and nobody had updated the document.

   When something breaks afterwards we do not know which change did it.
   There are a few hundred of them."
