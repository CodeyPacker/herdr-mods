  Management set goals:  "10 deployments a week" and
                         "change failure rate under 5%".

  Six months later:

  - Each release is split into several deployments of a few lines,
    made minutes apart. Deployments a week: 14.
  - Problems after a release are fixed quietly and not recorded as
    incidents. Change failure rate: 2%.
  - Customers report as many problems as before.
