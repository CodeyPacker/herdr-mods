  Summary

  A support engineer was asked to turn off "export-v2" for one customer.
  The flag tool lists 140 flags. They switched off "export_v2_enabled",
  which is a different flag: the global one, created three years ago
  and on for everyone since.

  With it off, every export used the old code path. That path had not
  run in three years and failed against today's database.

  Exports were down for 50 minutes.
